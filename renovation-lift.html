Renovation Lift Calculator

“Is the upgrade worth it?”

The value-add question on one screen: spend this much per suite, get this much more rent — what did that do to the building's value? It's the loss-to-lease math buyers run on your building. Run it first.

How this is calculated

All-in cost is renovation cost plus rent lost while suites sit empty. The annual rent lift is capitalized at the cap rate to give value created; net gain is value created less all-in cost. Yield on cost is the annual lift ÷ all-in cost.

What it leaves out

  • Financing costs during the programme, turnover costs and leasing time beyond the vacancy months entered.
  • Any change in operating expenses after renovation.

Assumes the full lift is achievable and capitalizes at the stated rate. Alberta has no rent control: one increase per 12 months with proper notice under the Residential Tenancies Act.
