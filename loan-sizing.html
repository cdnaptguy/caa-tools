Maximum Loan Sizing

“How much will they lend?”

Lenders size loans to whichever binds first — leverage or coverage. Enter NOI to see the maximum loan, which constraint binds, and how insured sizing compares to a typical conventional structure.

How this is calculated

The DSCR limit is the loan whose annual payment equals NOI ÷ the coverage floor, using Canadian semi-annual compounding. The LTV limit is lending value × maximum LTV. The DSCR limit includes the capitalized insurance premium: divide it by (1 + premium rate) to find net proceeds. The lender advances the lesser of that net limit and the LTV limit.

The conventional comparison runs the same test at SAMPLE terms (70% LTV, 25-year amortization, 1.25× DSCR, rate 0.75% above the insured rate) — edit them to match a real quote.

What it leaves out

  • Automatic premium pricing by LTV and program. Enter the applicable premium from the CMHC Premium Calculator or a lender quote, and recheck it if the resulting LTV changes. The Deal Financing Comparator also models premium bands.
  • Lender-specific underwriting of NOI, which is almost always lower than the owner's.
