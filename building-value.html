Building Value Estimator

“What's it worth?”

A quick income-approach estimate. Enter rents and expenses, pick a cap rate, and see value across a band either side of it — the same sanity check we run before any opinion of value.

How this is calculated

Gross potential rent is suites × average rent × 12. Vacancy and bad debt are taken off rent and other income to get effective gross income. Operating expenses (per suite × suites) come off that to give net operating income (NOI). Value is NOI ÷ cap rate, shown at the midpoint and at the band either side.

What it leaves out

  • Below-market rents (loss to lease) and the value a buyer may pay for that upside.
  • Deferred maintenance, capital needs and building condition.
  • Financing effects: buildings that qualify for CMHC-insured debt can attract buyers who price differently.

For the real thing we would use the CMHC Rental Market Survey, City of Edmonton or Calgary assessment data, and closed comparable sales.
