Multifamily Cash Flow Calculator

“What does it earn?”

Annual cash flow, debt coverage and cash-on-cash return on a purchase — using Canadian semi-annual-compounding mortgage math, not the U.S. formulas most online calculators quietly use.

How this is calculated

NOI is effective gross income (rent plus other income, less vacancy) minus operating expenses. Debt service uses Canadian semi-annual compounding: monthly rate = (1 + annual rate ÷ 2)1/6 − 1. Cash required is the down payment plus closing costs. Cash-on-cash is annual cash flow ÷ cash required; the paydown return adds year-one principal repaid.

Break-even occupancy is the share of potential income needed to cover operating expenses and debt service.

What it leaves out

  • CMHC premiums (see the CMHC Premium Calculator) and how insured debt is sized (see Maximum Loan Sizing).
  • Income tax, CCA, rent growth and capital spending after closing.
