MLI Select Points Estimator

“Do I qualify?”

MLI Select scores a building on affordability, energy efficiency and accessibility. Fifty points is the entry ticket; each tier up unlocks more leverage, longer amortization and a bigger premium discount.

How this is scored

Points from the three categories add up; a 20-year affordability commitment adds 30 bonus points to an affordability level. The total sets the tier: 50, 70 or 100 points.

Existing buildings, as summarized here

  • Affordability: 40% / 60% / 80% of units at rents at or below 30% of median renter income for the market = 50 / 70 / 100 points. Minimum 10-year commitment with capped rent increases.
  • Energy efficiency: 15% / 25% / 40% reduction in energy use = 20 / 35 / 50 points, validated by an energy professional.
  • Accessibility: 20 or 30 points for accessible or universal-design units meeting CSA standards.

CMHC has adjusted MLI Select criteria several times, including energy-code changes with a transition through September 30, 2026. Actual scoring depends on your market's median renter income, unit-by-unit rents, energy modelling and CMHC's current criteria.
