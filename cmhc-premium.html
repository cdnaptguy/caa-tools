CMHC Premium Calculator

“What does insurance cost?”

Estimate the mortgage insurance premium on a standard existing rental: a base rate set by loan-to-value, a surcharge for amortization beyond 25 years, and the MLI Select discount applied after surcharges.

How this is calculated

Premium rate = base rate for the LTV band + 0.25% for each 5 years (or part) of amortization beyond 25, then reduced by the MLI Select discount (10%, 20% or 30% for 50, 70 or 100 points). Premium = loan × net rate.

Default grid: 2.60% to 65% LTV, 2.85% to 70%, 3.35% to 75%, 4.35% to 80%, 5.35% to 85%, with MLI Select bands of 5.90% to 90% and 6.15% to 95% — the schedule for applications from July 14, 2025 as reported by industry sources. Every rate is editable so the page stays useful as CMHC updates pricing.

What it leaves out

  • CMHC application fees (per suite), non-residential space surcharges, second-mortgage and other surcharges.
  • Provincial sales tax on premiums in provinces that charge it (none in Alberta).
  • Construction financing and non-standard housing types, which price differently.

This is an estimate, not a quote. CMHC sets the final premium at underwriting.
