Sell vs. Refinance & Hold

“Cash out or keep it?”

The real decision long-term owners face. Compare after-tax cash from a sale against equity pulled out through a refinance — and the cash flow the building still throws off if you keep it.

How this is calculated

Sell: value less selling costs, the mortgage payout and your estimate of tax.

Refinance: the new loan is the lesser of the LTV cap and the loan NOI can carry at the lender's DSCR floor (Canadian semi-annual compounding). Cash out is the new loan less the existing balance and refinance costs. If you enter a CMHC premium it's added to the loan, which raises debt service.

What it leaves out

  • Investing the sale proceeds elsewhere, future appreciation and rent growth, and the eventual tax on a later sale.
  • Mortgage prepayment penalties on the existing loan unless you include them in selling or refinance costs.

Market context only — not legal or tax advice. Capital gains, CCA recapture, s.85 rollovers and estate freezes are accountant and lawyer territory; we're happy to work with yours. There is no 1031-style exchange in Canada.
