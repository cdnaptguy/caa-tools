Commercial Mortgage Calculator

“What's the payment?”

Payment, balance at term end and interest cost — with amortizations to 50 years, because MLI Select changed what's possible. Semi-annual compounding, the way Canadian lenders calculate fixed-rate mortgages.

How this is calculated

Canadian fixed-rate mortgages compound semi-annually, not monthly. The monthly rate is (1 + annual rate ÷ 2)1/6 − 1, and the payment is the standard annuity payment at that rate over amortization × 12 months. The balance at term end is what's left after the term's payments.

The “full amortization” interest assumes the same rate for the whole amortization, which never happens in practice — it's there to show the scale of the trade, not to forecast.

Assumes monthly payments and a fixed rate for the term. Lender conventions on payment dates and interest adjustment can move figures slightly.
